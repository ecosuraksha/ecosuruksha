About EcoSuraksha

A social enterprise that makes environment and health solutions simple enough for ordinary people and communities to use.

Who we are

An enterprise with a social and environmental purpose

EcoSuraksha has a six-year-old commercial identity, built around social and environmental causes. It works much like a social enterprise: it earns by making useful products and offering useful services, and it uses that work to support sustainable development.

Its professionals bring more than 30 years of experience in test kit design and development, especially in the water sector. That experience is built into every kit we make.

We deal in environment and health products and services, with a particular focus on simple, low-cost solutions that people at the base of the economic pyramid can actually use.

What we work on

  • Environment monitoring and management
  • Water and sanitation, water safety and security
  • Public health
  • Livelihood acceleration
  • Waste utilisation
  • Alternative building materials

What we do

Two things, each tailored to the customer: products we design and make, and services we deliver.

Products

Innovative, simple products for environment and health management, waste and alternative building materials. We carry out applied research and development, and assemble the products ourselves.

Our current range centres on water testing kits: the JalSuraksha Chloroscope, the JalSuraksha H2S Vial and the JalSuraksha Turbidity Tube.

Testing and advisory work, training and capacity building, surveys, project design and implementation, technology validation, business models for small enterprises, waste management and event management.

Our approach

We connect people, planet, technology and industry. We act as enablers: blending technologies and innovative processes, adapting and optimising them, and creating sustainable business models for small enterprises with help from our network of subject experts. Our work is rooted in action and applied research, and in demystifying science so that solutions are easy to understand and easy to scale.
