About EcoSuraksha

Simple solutions for environment and health

EcoSuraksha is a six-year-old, Noida-based social enterprise. We make and deliver environment and health products and services, from water-testing kits to training, awareness programmes, waste utilisation and project support, with a focus on solutions that ordinary people and communities can use.

Our professionals bring more than 30 years of experience in test kit design and development, especially in the water sector. We work across India with individuals, communities, NGOs, schools, small enterprises, governments, agencies and industry.

Three simple questions. Three simple tests.

Not sure where to begin? Start with the question you want answered.

Is the water properly disinfected?

Chlorine added to water keeps killing germs only while some of it remains. The JalSuraksha Chloroscope shows how much is left.

Use: JalSuraksha Chloroscope

See how it works

Is it free from harmful bacteria?

Fill the JalSuraksha H2S Vial with your water and wait. If the water turns black, harmful bacteria are present and the water is not safe to drink.

Use: JalSuraksha H2S Vial

See how it works

How clear is the water?

Cloudy water can shelter germs and make disinfection less effective. The JalSuraksha Turbidity Tube measures clarity on the standard NTU scale.

Use: JalSuraksha Turbidity Tube
