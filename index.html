Clear water is not always safe water

Water can look clean and still carry germs that cause diarrhoea, typhoid or jaundice. EcoSuraksha kits let you check on the spot — at home, in a school, in a village or at a treatment plant — without sending samples to a laboratory.

Three simple questions. Three simple tests.

Not sure where to begin? Start with the question you want answered.

Is the water properly disinfected?

Chlorine added to water keeps killing germs only while some of it remains. The Chloroscope shows how much is left.

Use: Jal≈Suraksha Chloroscope

See how it works

Is it free from harmful bacteria?

Fill the vial with your water and wait. If the water turns black, harmful bacteria are present and the water is not safe to drink.

Use: Jal≈Suraksha H2S Vial

See how it works

How clear is the water?

Cloudy water can shelter germs and make disinfection less effective. The tube measures clarity on the standard NTU scale.

Use: Jal≈Suraksha Turbidity Tube
